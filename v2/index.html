Lab811

Mechanics · Materials · Machine Learning

We develop predictive models for materials and structures by combining solid mechanics, computation, data-driven modeling, and experiment. Our work spans constitutive modeling, fracture and creep, inverse problems, architected materials, and machine learning for mechanics.

Explore our research Meet the group

Research

What we work on

01

Machine learning for mechanics

Operator learning, recurrent models for history-dependent behavior, generative design, and learned constitutive models deployed in finite-element workflows.

Learn more →
02

Failure & lifetime prediction

Fracture, damage, creep, viscoplasticity, localization, and probabilistic failure of structural and architected materials.

Learn more →
03

Experiments, inverse problems & design

Indentation, full-field measurements, additive manufacturing, Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, and experiment-informed model discovery.

Teaching & Mentoring

Mechanics education, research training, and preparation for independent technical work.

Current Teaching

ME 475 — Machine Design I
Southern Illinois University Carbondale · Fall 2026

Undergraduate machine design emphasizing mechanics-based analysis, material behavior, stress and deflection, failure criteria, and design of mechanical components.

Previous Teaching

MAE 101 — Statics and Strength of Materials
Instructor of Record · UCLA · Spring 2026

Core undergraduate mechanics covering equilibrium, internal forces, stress and strain, axial loading, torsion, beam bending, and failure concepts.

Research Mentoring

Lab811 students are trained to connect mechanics, computation, and data. Depending on the project, this can include continuum modeling, finite-element implementation, machine learning, uncertainty quantification, mechanical testing, digital image correlation, or additive manufacturing.

Prof. Zhang has previously mentored undergraduate researchers at Caltech, UCLA, and Northwestern across computational mechanics, machine learning for materials, and experimental characterization.

Graduate Topics

Continuum mechanics · elasticity · plasticity · fracture · finite elements · computational mechanics · plates and shells · inverse problems · uncertainty quantification · machine learning for mechanics

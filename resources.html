Resources

A curated reading list and toolbox I recommend to graduate students — the material I wish someone had handed me on day one.

Continuum mechanics

  • Abeyaratne, R. Lecture Notes on the Mechanics of Elastic Solids (free, MIT).
  • Kelly, P.A. An Introduction to Solid Mechanics (free lecture notes).
  • Bower, A.F. Applied Mechanics of Solids, CRC Press, 2009 (free online).
  • Anand, L. and Govindjee, S. Continuum Mechanics of Solids, Oxford, 2020.
  • Gurtin, M.E., Fried, E. and Anand, L. The Mechanics and Thermodynamics of Continua, Cambridge, 2010.
  • Spencer, A.J.M. Continuum Mechanics, Dover, 2004.
  • Maugin, G.A. Continuum Mechanics Through the Twentieth Century, Springer, 2013.

Elasticity

  • Atkin, R.J. and Fox, N. An Introduction to the Theory of Elasticity, Dover, 2005.
  • Timoshenko, S. and Goodier, J.N. Theory of Elasticity, McGraw-Hill, 1951.
  • Green, A.E. and Zerna, W. Theoretical Elasticity, Clarendon Press, 1960.

Plasticity

  • Lubliner, J. Plasticity Theory, Courier, 2008.
  • Simo, J.C. and Hughes, T.J. Computational Inelasticity, Springer, 2006.
  • Hill, R. The Mathematical Theory of Plasticity, Clarendon Press, 1950.
  • Yu, T. Plasticity, Higher Education Press, 1989.
  • Dunne, F. and Petrinic, N. Introduction to Computational Plasticity, Oxford, 2005.
  • de Souza Neto, E.A., Perić, D. and Owen, D.R. Computational Methods for Plasticity, Wiley, 2011.

Creep & variational methods

  • Frost, H.J. and Ashby, M.F. Deformation-Mechanism Maps, Pergamon, 1982 (free online).
  • Washizu, K. Variational Methods in Elasticity and Plasticity, 3rd ed., Pergamon, 1982.

Tensor analysis

  • Huang, K. Tensor Analysis, Tsinghua University Press, 1991.
  • Brannon, R.M. Curvilinear Analysis in a Euclidean Space, University of New Mexico, 2004.

Finite element method

  • Reddy, J.N. An Introduction to the Finite Element Method, McGraw-Hill, 2004.
  • Reddy, J.N. An Introduction to Nonlinear Finite Element Analysis, Oxford, 2014.
  • The Periodic Table of the Finite Elements.
  • Open-source codes: deal.II, FEniCS, MOOSE.

Indentation & contact mechanics

  • Johnson, K.L. Contact Mechanics, Cambridge, 1987.
  • Fischer-Cripps, A.C. Introduction to Contact Mechanics, Springer, 2007.
  • Tabor, D. The Hardness of Metals, Oxford, 1951.
  • Sneddon, I.N. The axisymmetric Boussinesq problem for a punch of arbitrary profile. Int. J. Eng. Sci., 3, 47–57, 1965.
  • Johnson, K.L. The correlation of indentation experiments. JMPS, 18, 115–126, 1970 (cavity expansion model).
  • Hertz, H. On the contact of elastic solids (1882); English trans. in Miscellaneous Papers, Macmillan, 1896.
  • Hill, R., Storåkers, B. and Zdunek, A.B. A theoretical study of the Brinell hardness test. Proc. R. Soc. A, 423, 301–330, 1989 (representative strain).
  • Doerner, M.F. and Nix, W.D. Interpreting depth-sensing indentation data. J. Mater. Res., 1, 601–609, 1986.
  • Pharr, G.M., Oliver, W.C. and Brotzen, F.R. On the generality of the stiffness–area–modulus relationship. J. Mater. Res., 7, 613–617, 1992.
  • Oliver, W.C. and Pharr, G.M. An improved technique for hardness and elastic modulus. J. Mater. Res., 7, 1564–1583, 1992; and refinements, 19(1), 3–20, 2004.

Micromechanics & materials science

  • Qu, J. and Cherkaoui, M. Fundamentals of Micromechanics of Solids, Wiley, 2006.
  • Mura, T. Micromechanics of Defects in Solids, Springer, 1987.
  • Weinberger, C., Cai, W. and Barnett, D. Elasticity of Microscopic Structures, lecture notes, Stanford ME340, 2005.
  • Callister, W.D. Materials Science and Engineering: An Introduction, Wiley, 2007.
  • Hull, D. and Bacon, D.J. Introduction to Dislocations, Elsevier, 2011.
  • Dieter, G.E. Mechanical Metallurgy, McGraw-Hill, 1986.
  • Porter, D.A. and Easterling, K.E. Phase Transformations in Metals and Alloys, CRC, 1992.
  • Meyers, M.A. Dynamic Behavior of Materials, Wiley, 1994 (shock physics).

Machine learning

  • Zhang, A., Lipton, Z.C., Li, M. and Smola, A.J. Dive into Deep Learning (free interactive book).
  • Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y. and Courville, A. Deep Learning, MIT Press, 2016 (free online).
  • Gradient-boosted trees, e.g. LightGBM.

Numerics & computing

  • ADMM: Prof. Boyd's page.
  • Kopp, J. Efficient numerical diagonalization of Hermitian 3×3 matrices. Int. J. Mod. Phys. C, 19, 523–548, 2008; optimized C/Fortran implementations.
  • Basic linear algebra subroutines: D. G. Simpson (NASA).
  • Remeshing: Radovitzky & Ortiz, CMAME, 187, 543–569, 2000; Afront.
  • Molecular dynamics: LAMMPS introductory book.
  • HPC guides: Princeton Research Computing, Caltech HPC, scheduler Rosetta stone (PBS/Slurm/LSF/SGE).

Everyday tools

  • LaTeX equations to images: KLatexFormula, IguanaTex (PowerPoint add-in), LaTeX to SVG.
  • Computer Modern fonts (cmr10, cmmi10): CTAN.
  • Reference management: Zotero, Mendeley.
  • Handwritten notes with PDF annotation: Xournal++.

First course & broader reading

  • Beer, Johnston, DeWolf and Mazurek. Mechanics of Materials, 8th ed., McGraw-Hill, 2019 — where undergraduates should start.
  • Gordon, J.E. Structures: Or Why Things Don't Fall Down, Da Capo, 2009.
  • Eberhart, M. Why Things Break, Crown, 2007.
  • Miodownik, M. Stuff Matters, Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, 2014.
  • Larry Gonick's non-fiction comics — for kids and teens.
  • Nielsen, M. Reinventing Discovery: The New Era of Networked Science, Princeton, 2020.
  • Huff, D. How to Lie with Statistics, Norton, 1993.
  • Emerson, R.W. Self-Reliance, 1841.

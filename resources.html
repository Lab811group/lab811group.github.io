Resources

A curated mechanics and computation reading list for students joining the group.

Continuum Mechanics

  • Abeyaratne, R. Lecture Notes on the Mechanics of Elastic Solids.
  • Bower, A.F. Applied Mechanics of Solids.
  • Anand, L. and Govindjee, S. Continuum Mechanics of Solids, Oxford, 2020.
  • Gurtin, M.E., Fried, E. and Anand, L. The Mechanics and Thermodynamics of Continua, Cambridge, 2010.

Plasticity & Inelasticity

  • Lubliner, J. Plasticity Theory.
  • Simo, J.C. and Hughes, T.J. Computational Inelasticity.
  • Dunne, F. and Petrinic, N. Introduction to Computational Plasticity.
  • de Souza Neto, E.A., Perić, D. and Owen, D.R. Computational Methods for Plasticity.

Finite Elements

  • Reddy, J.N. An Introduction to the Finite Element Method.
  • Reddy, J.N. An Introduction to Nonlinear Finite Element Analysis.
  • The Periodic Table of the Finite Elements.
  • Open-source frameworks: deal.II, FEniCS, MOOSE.

Indentation & Contact Mechanics

  • Johnson, K.L. Contact Mechanics.
  • Fischer-Cripps, A.C. Introduction to Contact Mechanics.
  • Tabor, D. The Hardness of Metals.
  • Oliver, W.C. and Pharr, G.M. Foundational papers on instrumented indentation.

Machine Learning

  • Zhang, A., Lipton, Z.C., Li, M. and Smola, A.J. Dive into Deep Learning.
  • Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y. and Courville, A. Deep Learning.

Computing & Research Tools

  • Version control: Git and GitHub.
  • Scientific computing: Python, MATLAB, C++, Fortran.
  • Machine learning: PyTorch, JAX, scikit-learn.
  • Mechanics software: Abaqus, ANSYS, COMSOL.
  • Reference management: Zotero or Mendeley.
  • Technical writing: LaTeX.

Principal Investigator

YZ

Yupeng Zhang

Assistant Professor · Southern Illinois University Carbondale

Prof. Zhang is an applied mechanician working on solid mechanics, computational modeling, machine learning for materials and structures, inverse problems, and experimental mechanics.

Before joining SIU, he conducted postdoctoral research at Caltech and Northwestern University and served as Instructor of Record at UCLA. He received his Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from Texas A&M University.

Join Lab811

Openings

We welcome students interested in mechanics, materials, computation, machine learning, and experiments.

Prospective graduate students

Students interested in Ph.D. research are welcome to contact the group. Funding and specific openings depend on current projects and availability.

Strong preparation in one or more of the following areas is useful: solid mechanics, finite elements, materials, scientific computing, machine learning, optimization, experimental mechanics, or additive manufacturing.

Undergraduate research

SIU undergraduates interested in research are encouraged to reach out. Projects can be computational, experimental, or a combination of both. Prior research experience is not required, but curiosity, reliability, and willingness to learn are essential.

What to send

  • A short description of your research interests and why they align with Lab811.
  • Your CV or résumé.
  • An unofficial transcript, if applicable.
  • For graduate applicants, any relevant papers, projects, code, or technical writing.

Collaboration

We are also interested in collaborations that connect mechanics with experiments, advanced manufacturing, materials characterization, scientific machine learning, or high-performance computing.

Please note that an expression of interest does not guarantee an available position or financial support.

Teaching & Mentoring

I hold CIRTL Associate certification (Fellow 2019, Senior Fellow 2020) in evidence-based teaching, and served as Executive Committee Co-Director of the Academy for Future Faculty at Texas A&M.

Courses taught

MAE 101 — Statics and Strength of Materials
Instructor of Record · UCLA, Spring 2026
